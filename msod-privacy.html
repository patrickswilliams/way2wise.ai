MSOD app privacy policy

What the MSOD app (msod.way2wise.ai) collects, why, who sees it, and how long it is kept.

Applies to: the MSOD app at msod.way2wise.ai
Last updated: November 27, 2026 (in-app notice version 11)
Contact: patrick@way2wise.ai

1. About the app

The MSOD app supports the MSOD alumni community. It holds the conference program, suggests sessions and conversation partners, and runs Open Space topics and conference trivia. It also helps a small group of alumni volunteers keep the community's alumni lists accurate. Everyone who uses an account sees a short data notice and agrees to it first. This page sets out the same commitments in more detail.

2. Information we receive from Google sign-in

You sign in with your Google account. The app asks Google only for the basic sign-in scopes (openid, email and profile). From these we receive:

  • your name and email address;
  • a Google account identifier, so we can recognize you the next time you sign in;

We do not receive your Google password. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google data.

How we use Google user data: only to sign you in, show you your own account, and give you access to the parts of the app your role allows. We do not sell it or use it for advertising. We do not use it to train AI models, and we do not transfer it to anyone except as described in section 6.

3. Other information we collect

  • What you tell us: how you are attending (in person or remote), your time zone, and your interests.
  • What you do in the app: sessions you save or join, suggestions you dismiss, Open Space topics you pitch, comments you send to an Open Space session, and your trivia answers along with the leaderboard name you choose.
  • What you capture: photos, voice memos and notes you add. Each one is private, seen only by you, unless you choose Share when you add it.
  • Buddy check-ins: if you're paired with a buddy, what you write or send in your daily check-ins (intentions, photos, voice memos, notes, questions and debriefs).
  • Your LinkedIn profile link and publications, if you add them yourself under "How you're attending" or confirm them (see the LinkedIn trial and your alumni record).
  • Whether you've confirmed which alumni record is yours, and any note you send the stewards about it.
  • Essential cookies: small cookies that keep you signed in and remember your trivia player. The app uses no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Server logs: ordinary technical logs, such as IP address and browser type, kept by our hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.

4. Alumni lists

Volunteer data stewards from the alumni community keep the community's existing alumni lists in the app. These lists can include a person's name, former name, cohort, graduation year, email addresses, thesis title, and contact preferences such as "no calls". Stewards use the app to find and merge duplicate entries.

  • Only signed-in stewards can see the lists. Every view and every change is recorded in an access log.
  • Stewards may download a list for Alumni Council work, such as reaching out to a cohort. The file is kept only in the Council's shared folder for stewards and deleted when the work is done. Every download is recorded, with how many people it held.
  • The uploaded lists are never edited. Every merge can be undone.
  • Nobody appears in an alumni directory unless they claim their own profile.
  • Apart from the trial below, stewards work only from the lists the community already holds. They don't search the web, LinkedIn or any other site for anyone.

Your alumni record

  • When you sign in, the app may ask "Is this you?" if your email is on exactly one record in the alumni lists. You see only that record's name, cohort, graduation year and thesis title, never anyone else's record.
  • If you confirm it, the stewards see your LinkedIn link and publications on that record, to keep the alumni lists accurate, and they may include them in a list they download. If you don't, nobody else sees them.
  • You can send the stewards a note about your record, or ask them to link it by hand. Only the stewards see your note.
  • If you import publications from ORCID, the app sends only the ORCID iD you type to ORCID, to read your public works, and saves only the works you tick.
  • You can unlink your record or remove your publications at any time. Deleting your account removes them all.

LinkedIn suggestions trial (ends January 31, 2027)

The Alumni Council approved a trial to help alumni connect their LinkedIn profiles to the registry.

  • In November 2026, a script sent the names of about 1,000 alumni (including former last names), each with the words "Pepperdine" and the program's name, to Serper, a company that runs Google searches for its customers, to find possible LinkedIn profiles. Serper handles those searches under its own privacy policy. Nothing was taken from LinkedIn itself.
  • Correction: until November 26, 2026, this page said the search covered 50 to 100 alumni and used Google's Custom Search API. A second, larger search was run on November 23, 2026, and both searches used Serper. We've corrected this page and the in-app notice.
  • Up to 100 possible profiles are suggested during the trial.
  • For a few alumni, the website they listed on their own groups.io profile is a LinkedIn profile. We may suggest that link too, in the same way.
  • Alumni Council stewards checked each possible profile by hand: whether the name fits, whether it shows the Pepperdine MSOD, and whether anything contradicts it. Profiles that didn't pass were deleted. No AI judges the matches.
  • Only the links of suggested profiles are kept. The search results' titles and descriptions, and possible profiles that weren't suggested, have been deleted.
  • A suggested profile is seen only by you and the stewards who checked it. It isn't shown in any directory, used for introductions or shared, and it doesn't go on your record unless you confirm "Yes, that's me".
  • If you say it isn't you, it's deleted and won't be suggested again. If you don't answer, it's deleted after 90 days or when the trial ends, whichever comes first.
  • The search isn't run on anyone outside the trial, and won't be run again without a new notice.

5. How we use information

  • To run the conference program and show you what is happening now and next, in your time zone.
  • To suggest sessions and match conversation buddies.
  • To run Open Space topics and trivia, and to help conveners write up their sessions.
  • To keep the alumni lists accurate, as described in section 4.
  • To keep the app secure and to prevent abuse.

Nothing is sold. Nothing you share in the app is used for advertising or to train AI models.

6. Who sees it

  • Conference organizers see what they need to run the event, such as sign-ups. They see attendance only as counts, never who attended what.
  • Other attendees see only what you choose to share, such as a topic you pitch, your trivia leaderboard name, or a photo, voice memo or note you share.
  • The conference gallery: what you share appears there for signed-in attendees, with your first name and cohort unless you choose not to show your name. You can delete it at any time, and organizers can hide anything shared.
  • Your buddy sees your name and cohort, not your email. What you write or send in your daily check-ins is seen only by your buddy, and only while you're paired. Organizers don't see it. You can delete anything you sent.
  • Open Space comments: a comment you send to a session is shown to signed-in attendees and read out in the room, word for word, with your first name unless you choose not to show it. You can delete it at any time.
  • Your LinkedIn link and publications: if you confirm your alumni record, the stewards see them on it (see your alumni record). Otherwise nobody else sees them. They go on an alumni directory profile only when the directory opens and you choose to show them.
  • Data stewards see the alumni lists, as described in section 4.
  • Open Space sessions on Pepperdine's Zoom run under Pepperdine's policies and may be recorded and transcribed there. Conference staff add a session's transcript to the app so the convener can write up the session. Speakers' names are replaced by numbers as it's added, the file itself isn't kept, and nothing from it is shared without the convener's approval.
  • Session write-ups: to draft a session's write-up, its transcript (with speakers' names replaced by numbers), the comments sent to it and photos shared from it go to Google's Gemini AI through the app's Google Cloud project, which doesn't use them to train models. The convener edits and approves the write-up before anyone else sees it.
  • Slido: some sessions use Slido, a Cisco service, for live Q&A and polls. The app shows Slido inside its pages, but questions, votes and poll answers you enter there go to Slido, not to the app, and are handled under Slido's privacy policy. You can ask questions anonymously.
  • EQ Coach: the app's Coach page gets its replies from the EQ Agent, a separate way2wise.ai coaching app. When you use it, what you type there, and the session's program listing (title, kind, time, location and description) if you started from one, pass through the app to the EQ Agent with an ID number for your account, never your name or email. The app doesn't keep your coaching conversations. The EQ Agent keeps them, and any tries you commit to, in a separate conference account under the EQ Agent's privacy policy, and deletes all conference accounts on November 12, 2027. Deleting your account in the app deletes them sooner.
  • Service providers: the app is hosted on Google Cloud, which stores the data on our behalf. Session write-ups are drafted with Google's Gemini AI through the same Google Cloud project. Sessions held online use Pepperdine's Zoom. Live Q&A and polls use Slido.
  • Legal requirements: we would disclose information only if required by law.

7. How long we keep it

  • Conference data is deleted about 60 days after the conference ends.
  • A LinkedIn link or publications you add or confirm, and the link to your alumni record, are kept with your account until you remove them or delete your account. A suggested profile you haven't confirmed is deleted as described in the LinkedIn trial.
  • You can delete your account at any time from "How you're attending" in the app. Your account and your answers are removed straight away.

8. Your choices

  • You can view and change what you have told us in the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account in the app, or ask us to by email.
  • If you appear in an alumni list and want your entry corrected or removed, email us.
  • You can also remove the app's access in your Google account under Security → Third-party connections.

9. Security

Data is stored on Google Cloud and encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to the alumni lists is limited to named stewards and recorded in a log.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects or who sees it, we will update this page and the in-app notice. Everyone will be asked to agree again before continuing.
