MSOD app privacy policy

What the MSOD app (msod.way2wise.ai) collects, why, who sees it, and how long it is kept.

Applies to: the MSOD app at msod.way2wise.ai
Last updated: November 5, 2026
Contact: patrick@way2wise.ai

1. About the app

The MSOD app supports the MSOD alumni community. It holds the conference program, suggests sessions and conversation partners, and runs Open Space topics and conference trivia. It also helps a small group of alumni volunteers keep the community's alumni lists accurate. Everyone who uses an account sees a short data notice and agrees to it first. This page sets out the same commitments in more detail.

2. Information we receive from Google sign-in

You sign in with your Google account. The app asks Google only for the basic sign-in scopes (openid, email and profile). From these we receive:

  • your name and email address;
  • a Google account identifier, so we can recognize you the next time you sign in;

We do not receive your Google password. We do not access your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google data.

How we use Google user data: only to sign you in, show you your own account, and give you access to the parts of the app your role allows. We do not sell it or use it for advertising. We do not use it to train AI models, and we do not transfer it to anyone except as described in section 6.

3. Other information we collect

  • What you tell us: how you are attending (in person or remote), your time zone, and your interests.
  • What you do in the app: sessions you save or join, suggestions you dismiss, Open Space topics you pitch, and your trivia answers along with the leaderboard name you choose.
  • Essential cookies: small cookies that keep you signed in and remember your trivia player. The app uses no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Server logs: ordinary technical logs, such as IP address and browser type, kept by our hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.

4. Alumni lists

Volunteer data stewards from the alumni community keep the community's existing alumni lists in the app. These lists can include a person's name, former name, cohort, graduation year, email addresses, thesis title, and contact preferences such as "no calls". Stewards use the app to find and merge duplicate entries.

  • Only signed-in stewards can see the lists. Every view and every change is recorded in an access log.
  • The uploaded lists are never edited. Every merge can be undone.
  • Nobody appears in an alumni directory unless they claim their own profile.

5. How we use information

  • To run the conference program and show you what is happening now and next, in your time zone.
  • To suggest sessions and match conversation buddies.
  • To run Open Space topics and trivia.
  • To keep the alumni lists accurate, as described in section 4.
  • To keep the app secure and to prevent abuse.

Nothing is sold. Nothing you share in the app is used for advertising or to train AI models.

6. Who sees it

  • Conference organizers see what they need to run the event, such as sign-ups. They see attendance only as counts, never who attended what.
  • Other attendees see only what you choose to share, such as a topic you pitch or your trivia leaderboard name.
  • Data stewards see the alumni lists, as described in section 4.
  • Open Space sessions on Google Meet are transcribed so that conveners can write up their session. Nothing from a transcript is shared without the convener's approval.
  • Service providers: the app is hosted on Google Cloud, which stores the data on our behalf. Sessions held online use Google Meet.
  • Legal requirements: we would disclose information only if required by law.

7. How long we keep it

  • Conference data is deleted about 60 days after the conference ends.
  • You can delete your account at any time from "How you're attending" in the app. Your account and your answers are removed straight away.

8. Your choices

  • You can view and change what you have told us in the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account in the app, or ask us to by email.
  • If you appear in an alumni list and want your entry corrected or removed, email us.
  • You can also remove the app's access in your Google account under Security → Third-party connections.

9. Security

Data is stored on Google Cloud and encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to the alumni lists is limited to named stewards and recorded in a log.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects or who sees it, we will update this page and the in-app notice. Everyone will be asked to agree again before continuing.
