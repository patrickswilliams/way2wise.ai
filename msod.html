The MSOD app

One place for the MSOD alumni community's conference program, Open Space sessions and alumni connections.

What it does

  • Shows the conference program and what's on now and next, in your own time zone, for people attending in person or remotely.
  • Suggests sessions and matches conversation buddies across cohorts.
  • Runs Open Space topics and conference trivia.
  • Helps volunteer data stewards keep the community's alumni lists accurate.

Signing in

The app is at msod.way2wise.ai. You sign in with Google, and the app receives only your name and email address. It is in testing at the moment and open to invited testers.

Privacy

Read the MSOD app privacy policy for what the app collects, who sees it and how long it is kept.
